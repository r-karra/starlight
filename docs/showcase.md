# Showcase

## Purpose
This document describes a concise prototype showcase for the Starlight Platform and the key story to present.

## Showcase story
A student uses Google XR smart glasses to capture a live lecture, generates a study summary through the companion system, creates a reminder on the phone, and later reviews the material on a laptop.

### Why this story
- It demonstrates the value of cross-device orchestration.
- It shows how the system reduces study stress and screen overload.
- It highlights a calm workflow with explicit consent and continuity.

## Demo flow
1. The student activates XR glasses during a lecture and records audio or captures notes.
2. The Android companion asks for permission to process the capture.
3. The system sends the selected content to AI services to create a concise summary.
4. The companion creates a study note and a reminder for review.
5. The laptop syncs the note and displays a deeper review page.
6. The student sees the summary, picks the next study task, and chooses a calm reflection prompt aligned with family or faith.

## Feature checklist
- [ ] XR lecture capture

## Showcase benefits
- demonstrates the core idea with one coherent user journey
- makes the prototype feel concrete and approachable
- emphasizes privacy, calmness, and meaningful value

## What to show
- the demo story in a short narrative paragraph or slide

- the user benefit: less stress, clearer study support, and smoother task continuity
- the first MVP features that are already in scope

## Notes
This showcase is intentionally focused. It is not a full product spec, but a clear demonstration of the first prototype’s value.
