# Phase 4: Research and OS direction

## Objective
Explore the longer-term research path and the feasibility of deeper platform customization.

## Goals
- Capture learnings from the prototype and identify where OS-level work could help.
- Investigate AOSP-based customization only after the core workflow is validated.
- Document the risks, opportunities, and next research questions.

## Milestones
- [ ] Review the prototype results and validate the interaction model.
- [ ] Identify candidate areas for deeper OS or AOSP-level exploration.
- [ ] Document the research questions for privacy, trust, and multimodal orchestration.
- [ ] Create a plan for future platform experiments and custom device work.

## Notes
This phase is research-first, not implementation-first. It should be driven by evidence from prior phases.
