# Validation plan

## Purpose
This document defines how to validate the Starlight prototype with real users and measurable outcomes.

## Success criteria
- Users feel less overwhelmed by screens and notifications.
- Students report better understanding of captured lectures and study material.
- Job holders report better task continuity and less distraction.
- Users trust the system’s privacy, consent, and device handoff behavior.
- The prototype supports calm, values-oriented reflection without adding distraction.

## Metrics
- perceived screen overload reduction
- task continuity satisfaction
- study comprehension improvement
- trust in privacy and control
- frequency and usefulness of calm reminders or reflection prompts

## Test scenarios
- Student lecture workflow: capture, summary, and follow-up review.
- Research workflow: ask a question through XR glasses and receive a refined explanation.
- Work workflow: create and resume a task across phone and laptop.
- Reflection workflow: receive a gentle prompt and choose an aligned action.

## Data collection
- short surveys after each scenario
- usage logs for handoff and sync behavior
- qualitative notes on stress, attention, and distraction
- privacy/confidence ratings for consent and sharing

## Evaluation approach
- run small pilot tests with representative users
- compare the prototype to baseline tools or current workflows
- iterate based on whether the prototype reduces cognitive burden and supports meaningful priorities
