# Personas

## Purpose
This document defines the key personas for the Starlight prototype and the scenarios they represent.

## Personas

### Student
- Age: 16–24
- Context: school or university student with heavy lecture and study demands.
- Pain points:
  - difficulty keeping up with lecture concepts
  - stress from studying and assignments
  - distraction from screens and notifications
- Needs:
  - quick capture of lecture content
  - concise summaries and key takeaways
  - task and reminder creation that stays in sync across devices
- Success criteria:
  - better concept understanding
  - reduced study friction
  - more time for family, faith, society, and nature

### Mid-age job holder
- Age: 25–45
- Context: working professional balancing projects, deadlines, and personal life.
- Pain points:
  - screen overload and fragmented attention
  - missed follow-up actions
  - difficulty switching from quick tasks to deeper work
- Needs:
  - fast task capture and follow-up
  - seamless handoff between glasses, phone, and laptop
  - calm reminders and reduced notification noise
- Success criteria:
  - smoother task continuity
  - fewer interruptions
  - more focused work and restful downtime

### Caregiver / infant support
- Age: 25–40
- Context: caregiver for infants or young children seeking a calm routine.
- Pain points:
  - high emotional and cognitive load
  - need for gentle support without extra screen burden
  - concern about technology disrupting calm family time
- Needs:
  - non-intrusive assistance
  - reliable reminders and context-aware support
  - a calm, unobtrusive interface
- Success criteria:
  - reduced cognitive burden
  - maintained calm family environment
  - trust in the system’s discretion and privacy

## Scenarios
- `Student`: capture the lecture on XR glasses, summarize with AI, create study tasks, and review on the laptop.
- `Job holder`: create a task by voice, sync it to phone and laptop, and resume focused work later without losing context.
- `Caregiver`: receive gentle reminders and appropriate support without intrusive notifications.
