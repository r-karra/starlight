# Features

## Purpose
This document lists prototype features by phase and clarifies MVP scope.

## Phase 1: MVP
- XR lecture/audio capture
- mobile companion consent flow
- AI summarization of captured content
- task and reminder creation on phone
- basic sync to laptop or cloud state
- student capture workflow validation

## Phase 2: Cross-device orchestration
- explicit handoff rules across glasses, phone, laptop, and cloud
- research assistance workflow via AI
- laptop review and deeper task continuity
- job-holder task progress workflow

## Phase 3: Stress reduction and reflection
- calm reminder and reflection prompts
- reduction of notification overload
- values-aligned reminders for family, society, nature, and faith
- trust and privacy feedback collection

## Phase 4: Research and OS direction
- evidence-driven AOSP or platform customization research
- evaluation of deeper OS-level capture and privacy control
- documentation of future platform experiments

## Notes
- Keep Phase 1 small and testable.
- Treat Phase 4 as research, not initial development.
- Mark all features as optional until the core workflows are validated.
